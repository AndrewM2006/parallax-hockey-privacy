Parallax Hockey Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Parallax Hockey ("the app") is an iPhone app that lets you log shots against a goalie and see a summary of the goalie's performance, including save percentage and Goals Saved Above Expected (GSAx). This policy explains what information the app and the advertising service it uses handle. It is provided by Andrew Monteith ("I", "me").

Information I collect

I do not collect, store, or receive any personal information from you. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics of my own, and no servers that receive your data.

The shots you enter during a game are held only on your device while you use the app. They are not sent to me or to anyone else, and they are discarded when you leave the results screen or close the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, an advertising service operated by Google LLC. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app may collect and use certain information from your device, including your device's advertising identifier or other device identifiers, your IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location), information about the ads you see and how you interact with them, and diagnostic and performance information such as crash logs and app launch time.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud, and, where permitted and where you have agreed, to personalize ads. Third parties, including Google and its advertising partners, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or other identifiers as a result of ad serving. I do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and Google's general Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the app shows a consent message before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice later where the app provides that option.
  • On iPhone you can control whether apps may track you across other companies' apps and websites in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • You can manage ad personalization in your Google account at adssettings.google.com.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because I do not collect or store personal information about you, there is no personal data held by me to access, correct, or delete. For data handled by Google through its advertising service, please use the controls and contact options described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, I will update this page and change the effective date above.
